Debian Neglect Explorer

What is this?

An interactive map of every Debian source package. Each dot is one package, positioned by how popular it is (x-axis, popcon votes from low on the left to high on the right) and how stale it is (y-axis, days since last upload or oldest open RC bug, with older/staler at the top).

What are the quadrants?

The dashed lines split the plot at the median, creating four zones: Popular & Neglected (high votes, long neglect) are the most urgent; Popular & Active are well-maintained; Niche & Neglected are low-traffic and stale; Niche & Active are small but cared for.

How to use it

  • Hover any dot for details (votes, installs, bugs, maintainer, VCS status).
  • Click a dot to open its tracker.debian.org page.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. The axes extend beyond the data.
  • Shift+drag to brush-select a region. The sidebar lists only those packages.
  • Search by package name. Accepts regex: ^alsa$ for exact, alac|7zip for multiple.
  • Y-axis switches between days since upload and oldest RC bug age.
  • Min vote slider filters out low-popularity packages.
  • Dot size slider adjusts circle radii by install count.
